Drought stress is a major cause of crop yield loss, yet the cell-type-specific responses of maize roots remain poorly understood. We performed single-cell RNA sequencing on 28,417 cells isolated from control and drought-treated maize roots to map transcriptional changes across major root cell populations. Comparative analysis identified strong activation of abscisic acid signaling, osmotic stress response genes, and cell-wall remodeling pathways in cortical and endodermal cells, while meristematic cells showed reduced expression of cell-cycle regulators. We also detected a drought-associated subpopulation of epidermal cells enriched for transporter genes linked to water and ion homeostasis. These results provide a high-resolution atlas of maize root adaptation to water limitation and highlight candidate pathways for improving crop resilience.
