We conducted a randomized controlled trial across six urban primary-care clinics to evaluate whether automated SMS reminders improve completion of a two-dose adult hepatitis vaccination schedule. Adults initiating vaccination were assigned either to standard care or to standard care plus reminder messages sent three days before and on the due date of the second dose. Among 1,248 participants, completion rates were significantly higher in the reminder group than in the control group, with the largest gains observed among adults younger than 40 years. Missed follow-up appointments were also reduced in the intervention arm. Automated reminders may represent a scalable, low-cost strategy to improve vaccine adherence in resource-constrained clinical settings.
